# Maintenance Windows & Escalation Contacts

## Maintenance Windows
* **Standard Production Window**: Every Sunday between 01:00 UTC and 04:00 UTC.
* **Emergency Window**: Allowed anytime with immediate CAB notification and approval from the Head of Engineering.
